Questions the office asks.

What can I store for each student?

Name, roll number, class and section, attendance, grades, and guardian contact — and the record holds whatever else your register needs, added as you go.

Can I edit a record after it’s created?

Yes. Open any student, change the field, save. The updated value is what everyone sees from then on — no hunting through versions.

What happens when I delete a record?

You’re asked to confirm first. Once confirmed, the record is retired cleanly — it leaves the active register without leaving partial entries behind.

Is this a spreadsheet?

No — it’s a real student database. Every student is one record, so a changed grade updates everywhere at once, and nothing depends on a fragile formula.

Can I find a student by roll number?

Yes. Search by name, roll number, or class — the matching record comes up instantly, however you usually think of them.
